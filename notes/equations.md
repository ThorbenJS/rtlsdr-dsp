## Mean (average) of a signal

$$
\mu = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} x_i
$$

$\mu$ is the mean, $N$ the number of samples, and $x_0$ to $x_{N-1}$ the
samples.

*Source: Smith, ch. 2, eq. 2-1.*

## Standard deviation of a signal

$$
\sigma^2 = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} (x_i - \mu)^2
$$

$\sigma$ is the standard deviation and $\sigma^2$ the variance, $\mu$ the mean
from eq. 2-1, $N$ the number of samples, and $x_i$ the samples.

*Source: Smith, ch. 2, eq. 2-2.*
