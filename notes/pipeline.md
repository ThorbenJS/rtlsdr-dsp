## 1. Initalize dongle
- First the dongle is to be set and initialized properly.
- The dongle needs to be set at some center frequency. The dongle will set the analog filter to take in signals 
around the center frequency at a range determined by the sample rate. -> range = center_freq +- sample_rate / 2.
